About the data (click to open or close) Level of activity = average daily entries (tap-ins). Exits at 32 Putrajaya Line stations are missing in the source data, so activity uses entries only and no destination line ends at those stations.

Surroundings: places and land use within 500 m, from OpenStreetMap (fetched 2026-09-30). Counts show what volunteers have mapped, not a census.

Station types compare entries with residents within 500 m (Kontur population, 2023). Stations with park-and-ride or feeder buses draw users from further away and can look like destinations.
