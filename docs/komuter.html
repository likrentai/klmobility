About the data (click to open or close) KTM Komuter trips by origin, destination and hour, 01 Jun 2026 to 31 Aug 2026 (61 working days, 31 weekend and public holiday days). Each trip has one hour, most likely when it started, so exits are shown in the hour the passenger boarded. Trips are kept if at least one end is in the Klang Valley; stations further away appear only as destinations.

Home or work uses the peaks (06:00 to 09:59 and 16:00 to 19:59) on working days. Stations where many passengers change to the LRT or MRT can look like work ends: passengers leave Komuter there in the morning.

Track works: the KVDT2 double-track upgrading has changed timetables since 2024; in 2026 the Port Klang to KL Sentral line closes daily from 11:00 to 15:00. Ridership on the affected sections has fallen sharply, so midday hours there reflect the timetable as well as demand.

Track: straight lines between stations in timetable order, not the real alignment.
